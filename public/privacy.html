Our Commitment to Privacy

At NotThisDate, we believe privacy is a fundamental right. We collect only what's necessary to provide you with a great scheduling experience.

What We Collect

For Calendar Creators: Email address (for authentication), calendar data you create.

For Participants: Name and unavailable dates you submit. No account required.

What We Don't Do

  • We don't sell your data
  • We don't show advertisements
  • We don't track you across websites

Your Rights

You can access, delete, or export your data at any time. Just delete your calendars to remove all associated data.
