How NotThisDate Works - Simple Group Scheduling in 3 Steps

📅

1. Create a Calendar

Set up your date range and add participants. Share a simple link - no signups required for your group members.

🚫

2. Mark Unavailable Dates

Everyone marks when they CAN'T make it. It's faster than traditional availability polls - no more back-and-forth messaging.

✅

3. Find the Best Date

Our visual availability heatmap instantly shows which dates work for everyone. Green means everyone's available!

Why Choose NotThisDate Over Doodle, Datumprikker, or WhenIsGood?

🚀 Reverse Scheduling Logic

Instead of marking when you're available, mark when you're NOT available. It's faster and more intuitive for group trip planning.

🔒 Privacy First

No tracking, no ads, no selling your data. Your group's availability information stays private and secure.

💯 100% Free

No premium tiers, no paywalls, no limits. All features are free forever for unlimited calendars and participants.

📱 No Account Required

Participants can submit their availability without creating an account. Just click the link and mark your dates.

🎨 Clean, Modern Design

Beautiful Stripe-inspired interface that's easy to use on desktop and mobile devices.

📊 Visual Heatmap

Instantly see group availability with color-coded dates. Darker means more people are unavailable.
